A Little Letter

My dearest love,

Happy birthday. I still think about how strange it is that we found each other in a Roblox lobby of all places, and how the first time I actually heard your voice on that first Discord call, something in me just knew you'd matter.

You became my best friend so fast it almost didn't feel real — you told me everything, the big things and the small things, and I never stopped wanting to be someone worth telling things to.

There was a stretch where that was hard. You were with someone else, and I sat with feelings I didn't say out loud, because what you needed from me then was a friend who showed up for you without conditions. So that's what I tried to be.

And then June 2026 happened, and somehow the boy from that Roblox lobby got to become yours. I don't think I've stopped being grateful since.

So today isn't just your birthday. It's another year of the story I'd choose again, every single time.

— me, completely yours

A few reasons, off the top of my head

🌸 the way you say my name when you're excited about something
🎧 every voice call that turned into 3am somehow
💌 how easy it's always been to tell you anything
🩷 that you stuck around long enough for this to happen
🗡️ your questionable but relentless demon slayer opinions
🎀 just... you, honestly
